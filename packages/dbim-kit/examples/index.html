Filter chips and scheme cards

DBIM A.5.2.1 — Schemes and Services · source: /offerings/schemes/

  • Mission

    PM Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan (PM-JANMAN)

    Saturating Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group (PVTG) families and habitations with housing, water, roads, health, education, power and livelihoods.

    Area & Infrastructure Development
    Read more
  • Mission

    Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan (DAJGUA)

    Whole-of-government mission to close infrastructure and service gaps in 63,843 tribal-majority villages through 25 interventions of 17 Ministries.

    Area & Infrastructure Development
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS)

    Quality residential schooling for ST children in remote areas, on par with Navodaya Vidyalayas, with focus on academics, sports and culture.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Centrally Sponsored

    Post Matric Scholarship for ST Students

    Financial support to ST students studying beyond Class X, paid directly to their bank accounts through DBT.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Centrally Sponsored

    Pre Matric Scholarship for ST Students

    Scholarship for ST students of Classes IX and X whose parental income is up to ₹2.5 lakh a year.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    National Scholarship (Top Class) for Higher Education of ST Students

    Full support for ST students admitted to premier institutes such as IITs, AIIMS, IIMs and NITs identified by the Ministry.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    National Fellowship for Higher Education of ST Students

    Fellowships for ST students pursuing M.Phil and Ph.D., managed end to end on the National Fellowship portal.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    National Overseas Scholarship for ST Students

    Financial assistance for selected ST students to pursue Master’s, Ph.D. and post-doctoral study abroad.

    Education & Scholarships
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Vikas Mission (PMJVM)

    Strengthens tribal entrepreneurship and livelihoods built on minor forest produce, farm and non-farm products. TRIFED is the nodal agency.

    Livelihood & Entrepreneurship
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Venture Capital Fund for Scheduled Tribes (VCF-ST)

    Concessional finance and incubation support for start-up ideas by ST youth, managed by IFCI Venture Capital Funds Ltd.

    Livelihood & Entrepreneurship
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Equity Support to NSTFDC and State STFDCs

    Equity from the Ministry funds concessional loans to eligible STs through NSTFDC and State channelising agencies.

    Livelihood & Entrepreneurship
    Read more
  • Special Area Programme

    Pradhan Mantri Adi Adarsh Gram Yojana (PMAAGY)

    Integrated development of villages with significant tribal population, implemented under Special Central Assistance to Tribal Sub-Scheme.

    Area & Infrastructure Development
    Read more
  • Grant

    Grants under Proviso to Article 275(1) of the Constitution

    100% annual grants to States to bridge the development gap between Scheduled Tribes and others.

    Area & Infrastructure Development
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Development of Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)

    Need-based, comprehensive development of PVTGs living in remote, scattered habitats while retaining their culture and heritage.

    Area & Infrastructure Development
    Read more
  • Grant

    Grants-in-aid to Voluntary Organisations working for STs

    Support to voluntary organisations delivering education, health and livelihood services in service-deficient tribal areas.

    Partnerships & Grants
    Read more
  • Central Sector

    Support to Tribal Research Institutes (TRIs)

    Funding State Tribal Research Institutes and Centres of Excellence as think tanks for evidence-based tribal development.

    Research, Culture & Health
    Read more
